Neuron Lab

Simulate single neurons and watch their membrane voltage change while you move the sliders.

Leaky integrate-and-fire neuron

How does this work?

The membrane charges up like a capacitor and leaks back toward rest. Whenever the voltage crosses the threshold, the neuron fires a spike: the voltage is reset and stays there for a short refractory period.

Assumptions and limits

  • The whole neuron is treated as one point with one voltage: no dendrites, no axon.
  • The membrane leak is constant. Real ion channels open and close depending on the voltage.
  • Spikes are not simulated, only their times. After each spike the voltage is simply reset.
  • Threshold and refractory period are fixed numbers, and there is no noise. Real neurons adapt and fire more irregularly.
  • The input is a current injected by an electrode, not input from synapses.
  • The default values are typical textbook values, not measurements of one particular neuron.
  • Computed with RK4 and a time step of 0.1 ms; spike times are interpolated between steps.
