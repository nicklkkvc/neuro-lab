Brain Lab

The worm C. elegans is the first animal whose complete nervous system was mapped, neuron by neuron. Here you'll explore that wiring diagram and simulate it.

In preparation. This lab follows after the Neuron Lab and Network Lab.

Planned

  • An interactive map of the connections between the worm's neurons
  • Network analysis: hub neurons and the shortest paths from sensing to moving
  • A simulation of the whole network, with every assumption stated on the page
  • Touch experiments and virtual removal of neurons
